Technical details

What the answer means

Every answer is one of these three.

  • Supported

    A DYCI post agrees with the message.

  • Contradicted

    A DYCI post says something different.

  • Not confirmed

    No DYCI post settles it yet. This does not mean it is false.

Always read the evidence

Under the answer you see the official post it came from, with its date and a link. Open it to be sure.

Tagalog or Taglish messages show the closest official posts to read, without a yes/no answer for now. Questions (“when are the midterms?”) show the matching post.

Picture check levels

Screenshots and posters get a level from 5 to 1.

  • Level 5: Matches DYCI

    Every statement read from the picture matches a DYCI post, and none conflicts.

  • Level 4: Mostly matches

    Some statements match a DYCI post and none conflicts; the rest couldn’t be checked.

  • Level 3: Can’t confirm

    Nothing clearly matches or conflicts. Often the text was hard to read, or DYCI hasn’t posted about it. This does not mean it is fake.

  • Level 2: Sketchy

    It looks official, but something in it doesn’t fit DYCI’s posts. Check DYCI’s page before sharing.

  • Level 1: Conflicts with DYCI

    A DYCI post clearly says something different.

About · Terms of Use · Privacy Notice

Google confirms who you are; this site never sees your password. It keeps your DYCI email, your name and when you signed in, so that only DYCI accounts can use it. It does not keep what you check unless you turn on history in your account.

By signing in you agree to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Notice.

Privacy & history

Keep a history of my checks

Off by default. When on, the messages and links you check are kept under My checks so you can see them again. Only you can see them. Pictures are never kept. The newest 50 are kept, each for 90 days.
