About DYCI Campus Claim Check

Messages about classes, suspensions, exams and events spread fast in group chats, and screenshots lose their date and source along the way. This checker lets anyone in the DYCI community compare what they heard with DYCI’s own official posts before passing it on.

How it works

  1. You send a message, a link or a screenshot. English, Tagalog or Taglish.
  2. It finds the closest official DYCI post among the announcements it has collected, and the one sentence in it that matters.
  3. It compares the two and answers Yes (a DYCI post backs it), No (DYCI’s post says otherwise) or Not confirmed yet, always showing the post, its date and a link.
  4. Safety checks run last. They hold back an answer when the post is about a different day, another school, an old announcement or a rumour, so it would rather say “not confirmed” than guess.

What it can and cannot do

  • It only knows what DYCI has posted. If DYCI has not announced something, the answer is “not confirmed yet”, which does not mean the message is false.
  • For Tagalog and Taglish messages it shows the closest official posts without a yes/no answer, because automatic checks in these languages are not reliable enough yet.
  • For pictures, it reads the text and checks whether the photo is one DYCI posted. It does not detect whether an image was made with AI.
  • It can be wrong. The official post shown with every answer is the final word: open it.
  • National holidays come from the official list (Proclamation No. 1006, s. 2025). DYCI’s own announcement always takes priority.
