1. What the checker is

A student research project of Fake News Verification System with Comparative Analysis of Multiple Machine Learning Algorithms for Dr. Yanga's Colleges, Inc. at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges, Inc. It compares messages with DYCI’s official posts. It is not an official DYCI service, and its answers are not official DYCI statements.

2. Answers are a guide, not a guarantee

  • Answers are produced automatically and can be wrong or incomplete. The official DYCI post shown with each answer is the authority: read it before acting on anything.
  • “Not confirmed yet” means no DYCI post settles the message. It does not mean the message is false.
  • Do not rely on the checker alone for decisions about classes, exams, payments or safety. Follow DYCI’s official announcements.
