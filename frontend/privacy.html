Privacy Notice

DYCI Campus Claim Check · Last updated [date]

This notice explains what personal information the DYCI Campus Claim Check collects, why, how long it is kept, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). The checker is a student research project at Dr. Yanga’s Colleges, Inc. (DYCI) that compares messages about campus announcements with DYCI’s own posts.

Who is responsible for your information

The project team of [thesis title / section], [names of the researchers], under the supervision of [adviser]. Contact: [project email]. For concerns with DYCI as an institution, you may also contact the DYCI Data Protection Officer at [DPO email, if available].

What we do not collect

  • Your Google password. Google confirms who you are; the checker never sees your password.
  • The messages and links you check, unless you turn on history. They are processed only to give you an answer and are not saved.
  • The pictures you check, ever. They are processed only to give you an answer and are not saved, even with history on.
  • Your location, your contacts, your photo from Google, or your device’s address in our records.

Posts collected from DYCI’s Facebook pages

To give answers, the checker compares messages with posts that DYCI’s official Facebook pages published themselves. It collects the text, date and link of those posts through Facebook’s official interface, with the permission of each page’s administrators. Some posts name people, for example awardees or officers, exactly as the page published them.

  • Only posts from August 1, 2024 on are kept. Older ones are not collected.
  • When a page deletes one of its recent posts, the checker removes its copy at its next hourly check.
  • The collected posts are kept until [end date, e.g. the end of the project / AY 2026–2027], unless DYCI takes over the system.
  • To ask for a post to be removed from the checker, contact us (see below).

Who receives your information

  • Google provides “Sign in with Google” under its own privacy policy.
  • Tailscale carries the encrypted connection from the internet to the checker’s computer.
  • We do not sell, share or publish your information. Account details are visible only to the project’s administrators.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Your account: until you delete it, or until [end date, e.g. the end of the project / AY 2026–2027], when all accounts are deleted.
  • My checks (only if history is on): the newest 50 per account, each for at most 90 days. You can delete one, or all, at any time; turning history off lets you delete them too.
  • Daily counts: 400 days. After that, each day is merged into its month’s totals (for example, “October 2026: 1,240 checks”), which are kept for long-term trends. Neither contains personal information.
  • Anything deleted (an account, a saved check, an expired sign-in or count) is overwritten in the database file, not just hidden. Nothing is archived or copied elsewhere, and the database is not part of any backup.

How we protect it

The information is kept in a database on the project’s computer, reachable only through the checker. Connections from the internet are encrypted (HTTPS). Only a hash of each sign-in session is stored, administrators cannot see anyone’s saved checks through the checker (the database file stays on the project’s computer, which only the project team can open), and an administrator can disable an account that misuses the service.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you may ask to know what information we hold about you, have it corrected, object to its processing, and have it deleted. You can delete your account yourself at any time: Account → Delete my account. This erases your name, email, sign-in record, history choice and saved checks. For anything else, contact [project email]. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Your consent

By signing in with your DYCI Google account, you agree to the processing described here. You can withdraw it by deleting your account.
